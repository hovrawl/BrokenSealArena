# Cup candidates (generated)

Generated by `scripts/cup-candidates.py` from the source tables of `~/alchemy` (branch `arena`).
Do not edit by hand; re-run the script. Enemy levels and stats are OUR enemy table's
(the Reloaded retune). `#n` is the enemy-table row, the number a `Formation.memberIds`
entry (and a ladder `ARENA_TEAM`) uses. The zone number is the row of `gEncounterZones`;
the formation number is the row of `gFormations`. The zone's own `level` is the party
level the stock game made it for; the mobs' levels are the enemy table's.

Evidence of area data: `encounter_zones.c` comments name each zone's place, `encounter_rules.s`
maps scene (and entrance) to zone, `world_map_encounter_rules.c` maps world-map region and
terrain to zone, and `Field_RequestBattleFromEncounterTable(zone, slot)` calls in `src/scene`
start the story's scripted battles (bosses). The grouping into regions below is the only
editorial layer (`REGIONS` in the script).
